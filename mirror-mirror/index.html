YOUR QUESTIONS

Good questions.

How will we know it helps?+

Choose a result before the test: fewer unneeded dress changes, more talks with interested guests, or less waiting for a fitting room. Compare it with what happens now. More views alone do not prove more sales.

Live or photo: what changes?+

Live moves with the customer in front of a camera. Photo makes a new still image from a photo they take or upload. Your brand can use one or both.

Does it come with a mirror or screen?+

Live needs a camera, a browser and a screen. We agree on the setup with your team. Photo try-on on your website does not need a mirror.

Can we use our dresses or new collection?+

We start with a few of your pieces and check the results together. Details, fabric and shape can differ in an AI view, so we review them before agreeing on a wider test.

Can we use it on our website?+

We check your website first. Then we can tell you if photo try-on can work there and what needs to be done.

Does this replace trying the real clothes?+

No. It helps people explore a look. A bride still needs to feel the dress; a shopper still needs to check size and fit. AI can change colors and details. The preview should help choose what to try, not add a step with no benefit.
